About the voyage

Dalmatia — from the water

Late September is the best time for the Adriatic: the summer crowds have dispersed, the sun is soft and gentle, and the sea has warmed over the summer to an ideal temperature. Our route is a harmonious balance between quiet ancient towns, the atmosphere of la dolce vita and the absolute silence of wild turquoise coves. A comfortable sailing catamaran gives maximum space, stability on the water and lets us reach shallows that ordinary yachts can't.

  • Turquoise lagoons like the Caribbean. Palmižana in the Pakleni Islands, the Blue Lagoon between the Drvenik islands and the famous Zlatni Rat on Brač.
  • Ancient Dalmatian towns. White-stone Milna, ancient Stari Grad founded by the Greeks, and the fortress above the town of Hvar.
  • Wild coves and nights under the stars. Anchoring in sheltered bays — swimming straight off the deck and complete silence.
  • Authentic cuisine. Dinners in local konobas: the freshest seafood and famous Dalmatian wine.
  • A comfortable catamaran. Spacious cabins, a large saloon and a sunbathing net on the bow; short crossings with no rocking.
  • An autumn Adriatic without the rush. No heat, no crowds — an intimate group of 6–12 and a fully relaxed pace.

Route

Day-by-day programme

The voyage begins in ancient Split. After meeting the crew and the catamaran, we raise the sails and set a course for the island of Šolta. We spend the first evening in a quiet sheltered bay by a sunken old barge: swimming in clear water, a sunset and the first night under a starry sky in complete silence.

After a morning swim straight off the deck we head to the island of Brač — into a deep, sheltered bay to the old town of Milna. Time seems to stand still here: white-stone architecture, narrow lanes and fishing boats. In the evening — an authentic local konoba with the freshest seafood and famous local wine.

We cross to the legendary island of Hvar — but not to the noisy party spots, rather to the town of Stari Grad, one of the oldest settlements in Europe, founded by the ancient Greeks. An incredible calm reigns here: stone-paved streets, the old fortified villa of a local poet and the scent of pines and lavender that the whole island breathes.

A day devoted entirely to rest, swimming and paddleboarding. We head for the Pakleni archipelago — a lacework of tiny green islets in crystal-clear water. We put in at the turquoise bay of Palmižana with its remarkable botanical garden. A night at anchor in a quiet lagoon to the sound of the waves.

In the morning we climb to the old fortress above the town of Hvar for panoramic shots of the whole archipelago. Then a course to the southern shore of Brač — to the famous Zlatni Rat (Golden Horn) beach. In autumn this unique pebble spit, which shifts its shape with the currents, becomes calm and free of the crowds.

We head back towards the mainland along rocky shores, stopping on the way for a swim by a submerged military bunker in a wild cove. In the evening Maslinica awaits — the most picturesque village on the island of Šolta, with a grand 18th-century castle. Here, on a terrace by the sea, we'll watch one of the most beautiful sunsets on the whole Adriatic.

The final chord — the famous Blue Lagoon between the Drvenik islands: thanks to its sandy bottom the water here has a dazzling turquoise hue, just like the Caribbean. The perfect place for a final lunch, some swimming and vivid photo sessions. In the evening we return to port and hold a farewell dinner in the historic centre.

A morning breakfast on board and disembarkation in Split. Airport transfer by arrangement. Until we meet again on the water!
