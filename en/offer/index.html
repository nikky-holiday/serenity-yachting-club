Public offer for maritime tourism, charter and yachting-training services

Contractor: Sole proprietor K. A. Kudrina-Kuchuhurna · TM “Serenity Yachting Club” Revision of: 31 July 2026
This English text is a translation provided for convenience. The legally binding version is the Ukrainian original; in case of any discrepancy, the Ukrainian version prevails.

This document is an official public proposal (offer) by “Serenity Yachting Club” (hereinafter — the Contractor) to conclude a Contract for the provision of services organising yacht tours, chartering vessels (with and without a skipper) and training under yachting programmes with any natural or legal person (hereinafter — the Client).

Pursuant to Articles 633, 638, 641, 642 of the Civil Code of Ukraine, upon acceptance of the terms of this Offer (acceptance), the person becomes a Party to this Contract and undertakes to comply with all its provisions.

1. Terms and definitions

1.1. Contractor — the company “Serenity Yachting Club”, which provides leisure-organisation, charter and training services.

1.2. Services — include, but are not limited to:

  • organising yacht tours, cruises and regattas;
  • renting (chartering) yachts with a captain (crew charter);
  • self-skippered yacht rental without a captain (bareboat charter);
  • skipper-training courses for private sailing vessels under the international IYT and ISSA systems and the national programme of Ukraine.

1.3. Website — the official web resource: https://serenityyachting.club.

1.4. Bareboat charter — the transfer of a vessel for temporary use to a Client who holds the relevant confirmed qualification documents.

2. Subject of the Contract

2.1. The Contractor undertakes to provide the Client with the chosen Services (a tour, yacht rental or a training course), and the Client undertakes to pay for them on time and to comply with the terms of this Contract, as well as the international and national rules of maritime safety.

3. Booking and the procedure of Acceptance

3.1. Acceptance of this Offer is the Client's payment of a deposit or of the full price of the Services on the Website or to the Contractor's requisites.

3.2. A booking of a tour, a yacht or a place on a training course is secured for the Client after a deposit of 50% of the total price is paid. Full payment is made no later than 30 days before the start of the services.

4. Special terms for bareboat charter

4.1. To arrange a bareboat charter, the Client must provide valid international or national skipper certificates (IYT, ISSA, RYA certificates or a national licence), as well as a radio-operator certificate (VHF/SRC) meeting the requirements of the sailing area.

4.2. Security deposit: for a self-skippered charter the Client undertakes to pay a refundable security deposit before putting to sea. In case of damage to the vessel, loss of equipment or delay in returning the yacht, the losses are compensated from this deposit.

4.3. The Contractor or the vessel-owning company has the right to refuse a bareboat charter during the vessel check-in if the Client's actual skills are insufficient for safe handling, offering the hire of a professional skipper at the Client's expense.

5. Special terms for training and certification (IYT, ISSA, national licence)

5.1. The Contractor provides theoretical and practical training in accordance with the standards of the chosen system (IYT, ISSA or the national programme of Ukraine).

5.2. Successful payment for a course does not guarantee automatic issue of a certificate/licence. Documents are issued only upon successful passing of the examinations (theoretical and practical).

5.3. Production and delivery of official international plastic certificates is paid at the rates of the relevant organisations (IYT/ISSA), unless included in the base price of the course.

6. Rescheduling, cancellation and weather conditions

6.1. Weather conditions: during tours, charters or practice, the decision to put to sea is taken solely by the Captain/Instructor. In case of unfavourable weather (a storm, dangerous wind), the departure time or route may be changed or rescheduled to another time/date without financial penalties for the Parties.

6.2. Cancellation at the Client's initiative. Refunds are made upon a written request to mykola@serenityyachting.club:

  • cancellation 60 or more days before the start — 100% of the amount paid is refunded (less bank charges);
  • cancellation from 59 to 30 days before the start — 50% of the total price of the Services is refunded;
  • cancellation less than 30 days before the start — no refund is made, or rescheduling terms are discussed individually.

7. Safety rules and liability

7.1. While on board, all participants must unconditionally follow the instructions of the Captain/Instructor.

7.2. The Client is solely responsible for holding valid passports, visas and medical insurance (including cover for extreme sports/yachting) for travel abroad.

8. Force majeure (circumstances of insuperable force)

8.1. The Parties are released from liability for failure to perform or improper performance of obligations under this Contract in the event of circumstances of insuperable force beyond the Parties' control (natural disasters, storm warnings, unfavourable hydrometeorological conditions, military action, the introduction of martial law or a state of emergency, closure of sea areas, decisions of port or border services, epidemics, border closures, etc.).

8.2. The Party affected by force majeure must notify the other Party in writing within 3 (three) business days.

8.3. In the event of force majeure, the deadline for performing obligations under the Contract is postponed for the duration of such circumstances, or the service is moved to another agreed date without penalties.

9. Dispute resolution

9.1. All disputes, disagreements or claims arising out of or in connection with this Contract, including as to its performance, breach, termination or invalidity, the Parties undertake to resolve through negotiations and written claims.

9.2. The claims procedure is mandatory. The period for considering a written claim is 14 (fourteen) calendar days from the moment it is received by the other Party.

9.3. If the Parties do not reach agreement through negotiations, the dispute is subject to judicial consideration under the current legislation of Ukraine in accordance with the jurisdiction determined by law.

9.4. The substantive and procedural law of Ukraine applies to the relations under this Contract, as well as international maritime customs and conventions in the case of tours/charters outside Ukraine.

10. Term of the Contract and changes to the terms

10.1. This Contract enters into force from the moment of the Client's Acceptance (payment/deposit) and is valid until the Parties have fully performed their obligations.

10.2. The Contractor has the right to unilaterally amend the text of this Offer by publishing a new version on the Website. Changes take effect from the moment of publication, unless the new version of the Offer provides otherwise.
