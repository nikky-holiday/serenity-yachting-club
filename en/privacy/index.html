Privacy and personal-data processing policy

Data controller: Sole proprietor K. A. Kudrina-Kuchuhurna · TM “Serenity Yachting Club” Revision of: 31 July 2026
This English text is a translation provided for convenience. The legally binding version is the Ukrainian original; in case of any discrepancy, the Ukrainian version prevails.

This Privacy and personal-data processing policy (the “Policy”) defines the procedure for obtaining, collecting, accumulating, processing, storing, using, changing, destroying and protecting the personal data of users of the website of “Serenity Yachting Club” (the “Contractor” or “We”).

We respect the confidential information of any person who visits our website, seeks a consultation, orders yacht-rental services or yacht tours, or trains under the IYT, ISSA programmes or a national licence of Ukraine. Accordingly, We strive to protect the privacy of personal data, creating comfortable and secure conditions for using our Website.

This Policy is developed in accordance with the requirements of the Law of Ukraine “On the Protection of Personal Data”, the Law of Ukraine “On Electronic Commerce”, and taking into account the general principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Terms and definitions

  • Personal-data database — a named set of ordered personal data in electronic form and/or in the form of card files.
  • Personal-data owner — the company “Serenity Yachting Club”, which determines the purpose of personal-data processing and establishes the composition of this data and the procedures for its processing.
  • User (Client) — any natural person who visits the Website, orders yachting services, books tours or charters, or undertakes training.
  • Personal data — information or a set of information about a natural person who is identified or can be specifically identified.
  • Website — the official website of “Serenity Yachting Club”.

2. Composition and scope of the personal data processed

We process only the personal data that is necessary for the proper provision of maritime-tourism, charter and yachting-training services:

3. Purposes of personal-data processing

Users' personal data is collected and processed for the following purposes:

  • Ensuring the fulfilment of civil-law, tax and accounting relations.
  • Providing services organising yacht tours, cruises, vessel rental and training courses.
  • Identifying the Client when placing an order, registering for training or putting to sea.
  • Submitting information to international accreditation organisations (IYT Worldwide, International Sailing Schools Association — ISSA) to produce and register official certificates.
  • Submitting crew lists to marinas, port administrations, the coast guard and border services.
  • Arranging insurance policies for tour and practice participants (where necessary).
  • Sending service notifications, class schedules and organisational information about departures.
  • Sending marketing materials, special offers and Service news (only with the Client's consent).

4. Personal-data retention period

Personal data is stored for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of its processing set out in section 3 of this Policy, but no less than the period established by the current legislation of Ukraine for storing primary accounting and tax documents.

Data on the successful completion of training and issued certificates (IYT, ISSA, national licences) is stored indefinitely in the “Serenity Yachting Club” archive to make it possible to restore or confirm the Client's qualification in the future.

5. Transfer of personal data to third parties

We do not sell or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial purposes. Transfer of personal data to third parties is permitted only in the following cases:

  • To accredited international training organisations: transfer of students' data to IYT or ISSA to issue and verify skipper certificates.
  • To port and state authorities: transfer of crew lists and captains' documents to port services, the coast guard, border and customs authorities in accordance with maritime law and the legislation of the country of stay.
  • To charter companies and vessel owners: when arranging a bareboat charter to confirm qualifications and obtain vessel clearances.
  • To insurance companies: to arrange mandatory or voluntary insurance during sea voyages.
  • By requirement of law: transfer of data at the request of Ukrainian state authorities in the cases and manner provided by the legislation of Ukraine.

6. Rights of the User (data subject)

Under Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine “On the Protection of Personal Data”, the User has the right to:

  • know the sources of collection and the location of their personal data, and the purpose of its processing;
  • receive information about the conditions of access to personal data, including information about third parties to whom their data is transferred;
  • access their personal data;
  • receive, no later than thirty calendar days from the date of the request, a response as to whether their personal data is being processed, and receive the content of such data;
  • submit a reasoned demand to the personal-data owner objecting to the processing of their personal data;
  • submit a reasoned demand to change or destroy their personal data if it is processed unlawfully or is inaccurate;
  • have their personal data protected from unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction or damage;
  • use legal remedies in case of a breach of personal-data legislation;
  • withdraw consent to the processing of personal data (where processing is based on consent).

7. Use of cookies and analytics services

Cookies are small text files stored on the User's device when visiting the Website. We use cookies to:

  • ensure the correct operation of the Website's functionality and booking forms;
  • analyse traffic and user behaviour using web-analytics services (Google Analytics, etc.);
  • save the User's individual settings.

The User may at any time disable cookie storage in their browser settings, although this may limit the functionality of some sections of the Website.

8. Protection of personal data

“Serenity Yachting Club” takes all necessary organisational, technical and legal measures to protect Users' personal data from unauthorised or accidental access, destruction, distortion, blocking, copying, distribution and other unlawful acts of third parties.

Access to personal data is granted solely to authorised employees and instructors who have signed non-disclosure undertakings.

9. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to unilaterally amend this Policy. A new version of the Policy takes effect from the moment it is published on the Website, unless the new version provides otherwise.
