About the voyage

All the gems of the Amalfi Coast

Italy's most beautiful coast reveals itself best from the sea. We start from Naples and unhurriedly round the capes of Amalfi, stopping where the shore crowds are left behind — from the grottoes of Capri to the thermal coves of Ischia.

  • Capri and the Blue Grotto. The Faraglioni rocks, swimming in clear water and a morning coffee while the island still sleeps.
  • Positano from the sea. Colourful houses cascading down to the water, and an evening aperitif on deck.
  • Ischia and Procida. Thermal coves, warm volcanic water and a colourful fishing town.
  • Amalfi and Ravello. St Andrew's Cathedral, lemon terraces and coastal panoramas.
  • The taste of Campania. Limoncello, mozzarella di bufala and dinner in a family trattoria.
  • An intimate group of 6–12. Maximum privacy and a comfortable pace.

Route

Day-by-day programme

Crew welcome, settling into cabins and getting to know the catamaran. An intro briefing and the first evening on board with a view of Vesuvius.

The Faraglioni rocks, the famous Blue Grotto and swimming in clear water. A morning coffee while the island still sleeps.

Colourful houses cascading down to the sea. A walk through the lanes and an evening aperitif on deck.

The heart of the coast: the majestic St Andrew's Cathedral, narrow alleys, limoncello and lunch in a local trattoria.

A calmer stretch of the coast: swimming in sheltered coves, homemade pasta and rest away from the crowds.

The green volcanic island: warm thermal coves, relaxing in natural springs and dinner with a view of the Aragonese Castle.

The most photogenic island of the bay: the pastel facades of the fishing harbour, a final swim and a farewell dinner on board.

A morning return to the marina, breakfast and disembarkation. Transfer to the airport by arrangement.
