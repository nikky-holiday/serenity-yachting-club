About the voyage

The Lipari (Aeolian) Islands — from the water

September in Sicily and the Liparis is the velvet season at its best: the sea warmed to its peak, the summer marina rush fading, and gentle sun creating ideal conditions for a holiday at sea. We set off from the Capo d'Orlando marina on a spacious catamaran that opens new horizons every day.

  • Daily swims in wild coves. Bays of the archipelago you can't reach from land: crystal water, grottoes and SUP.
  • Stromboli's night show. Dinner on deck to the rumble and flashes of the Sciara del Fuoco lava against a starry sky.
  • The taste and aesthetics of Salina. A tasting of Malvasia wine and dinner in a family restaurant overlooking the sea.
  • Snow-white Panarea. The most stylish island of the archipelago — white architecture and atmospheric lanes.
  • Vulcano and Lipari. A climb to the crater, relaxing in sulphur springs and the old fortress town.
  • An intimate group of 6–12. Maximum privacy, no crowds and a comfortable pace.

Route

Day-by-day programme

Crew welcome, settling into cabins and getting to know the catamaran. First evening on board, an intro briefing and an aperitif at sunset.

Crossing to the archipelago. The Martian landscapes of Vulcano island, relaxing in warm sulphur springs and the first swim in clear coves.

A walk through the old fortress town, viewing terraces and cosy lanes. In the afternoon — a stop in a scenic cove for swimming.

The green pearl of the archipelago. A tasting of local Malvasia wine and dinner in a family restaurant with a sea panorama.

The most stylish of the Aeolians: white architecture, lounge bars and unhurried evenings. Swimming in the coves around the neighbouring rocks.

An evening anchorage at a safe distance from the active volcano. Dinner on deck under the flashes of the Sciara del Fuoco and a starry sky.

A full day on the water: the archipelago's most beautiful bays, swimming straight off the stern, SUP and grottoes. Farewell dinner on board.

A morning crossing to the marina, breakfast and disembarkation. Transfer to the airport by arrangement.
