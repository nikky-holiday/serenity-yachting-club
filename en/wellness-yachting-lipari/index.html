Wellness retreat · Aeolian Islands

Wellness catamaran retreat: yoga, the sea and a reset on the Liparis

October, a warm sea and the Aeolian Islands — the perfect setting to slow down: sunrise yoga, mindful eating with a nutritionist, and love for yourself and the world around you.

Dates: 03–10 October Duration: 8 days / 7 nights Vessel: sailing catamaran, 6–12 people Price: €2,900 / cabin · ≈ €1,450 per person

About the retreat

A retreat on the water — a return to yourself

This voyage blends yachting with practices that bring you back to yourself. The sea becomes the main therapist: morning yoga on deck, mindful eating, meditations under the stars — and no rush at all. You rest your body and your mind at the same time.

  • Morning yoga on deck. Asanas and breathing practices at sunrise, while the sea is still asleep.
  • Nutrition with a nutritionist. A look at your individual diet and the seasonal produce of Sicily — mindful and delicious.
  • The sea as therapy. Swimming in wild coves, SUP and slow sailing crossings.
  • Evening meditations. Gratitude practices and quiet under the flashes of Stromboli against a starry sky.
  • Love for yourself and the world. Space to slow down, hear yourself and come back renewed.
  • An intimate group of 6–12. A cosy atmosphere of like-minded people and maximum privacy.

Route

Day-by-day programme

Crew welcome, settling into cabins, getting to know the group. An evening grounding practice and a light dinner on board.

Morning yoga on deck, the warm sulphur springs of Vulcano and the first swim. An introduction to the principles of mindful eating.

Breathing practices at sunrise, a walk through the old town and a swim in a quiet cove. Dinner from seasonal local produce.

A day about taste and balance: a diet review with the nutritionist, the local produce of the green island and dinner overlooking the sea.

Yoga at sunrise, a slow pace and swimming around the snow-white island. Time for yourself and for quiet.

An anchorage near the active volcano. Meditation and a gratitude practice under the flashes of Stromboli against a starry sky.

A full day on the water: swimming, SUP, quiet and a final shared practice. A farewell dinner on board.

A final morning practice, breakfast and disembarkation. Transfer to the airport by arrangement.
