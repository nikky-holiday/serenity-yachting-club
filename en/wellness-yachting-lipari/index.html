On a catamaran our home travels with us: tonight you fall asleep off Vulcano, tomorrow you wake up by Lipari, and on the way to Stromboli you simply lie on deck and watch a new island rise from the horizon. Life on board

What is life on a catamaran like?

If you have never sailed, the word “cabin” sounds more austere than it actually looks. A catamaran is a wide twin-hull boat with proper living spaces, where you won't spend seven days on top of each other.

Double cabins

Your own private space for sleeping, your things and the moments when you just want to close the door and be alone.

Bathroom and shower

A proper bathroom with toilet, basin and shower in the cabin — no survival mode.

Saloon and galley

A shared space for breakfasts, coffee and conversation. This time the galley is safely left to our cook.

Open-air dining

Long breakfasts after yoga, lunches with island views and evenings when nobody wants to leave the boat.

Together — with somewhere to escape to

  • Want company — join the girls at the big table.
  • Want sun — head to the foredeck or the net above the water.
  • Want to read — take a book into the shade.
  • Want an afternoon nap — back to your cabin.
  • Want to sunbathe — find your own patch of deck.
  • Want to stay quiet and watch the sea — there is room for that too.

A women's voyage doesn't mean “30 people together 24/7”. We'll meet a lot at practices, on the islands and over dinners — but everyone keeps the option to live this week at her own pace.

Day by day

Seven days at sea

Capo d'Orlando → Vulcano. Boarding the catamarans, meeting the skipper and crew, a welcome table on board. Out to sea, a first swim at Porto di Ponente with its black volcanic sand, and sunset. In the evening — a seaside restaurant and the opening ritual: everyone chooses her word for the week and receives a keepsake.

Vulcano → Lipari. A gentle sunrise practice, then the climb to Gran Cratere (~2 hrs through volcanic landscape with fumaroles). The DREAM practice about big desires. A swim at wild Praia di Vinci facing the Faraglioni, an evening walk through old Lipari, the Citadel and Marina Corta. Optional yoga nidra or sound practice.

Lipari → Panarea. Sunrise yoga at a panoramic spot on Lipari. Snorkelling in Cala Junco. H&W talk “Biohacking for normal people”: sleep, energy, recovery, circadian rhythms, stress. Panarea — cacti, white houses, coffee and gelato. Aperitivo facing Stromboli and, in the evening, yoga nidra with Katia.

Panarea → Stromboli. Sunrise yoga and a swim by Basiluzzo. The climax: climbing active Stromboli with a licensed guide, sunset and the RELEASE ritual. For everyone else — watching the Sciara del Fuoco from the water. Dinner on the catamarans, a short meditation, the sea, the stars and Stromboli.

Stromboli → Salina. A restorative morning and a swim at Pollara by the Il Perciato arch. Salina's local bakery. H&W talk “The psychology of eating”. Free time: scooters, capers, Malvasia. In the evening — a short meditation and a calm night.

Salina → Lipari · Spiagge Bianche. A long stop by turquoise water: swimming, SUP, napping on the net, photos, sunbathing. Nail-board standing for those who want it. In the evening — a Chinese tea ceremony and a women's circle about female friendship after 30, closeness and support.

Lipari/Vulcano → Capo d'Orlando. A last sunrise and yoga, a swim by the Faraglioni di Lipari. H&W talk “Staying young after 30”. Back to Capo d'Orlando, a final dinner all together and the closing circle — returning to the word chosen on day one. The finale: sound healing.

A calm morning with optional short yoga. Breakfast together, packing, photos, hugs and a last coffee. Everyone gets a farewell snack box for the road. Home you go — with sea salt in your hair and a red bracelet on your wrist.
