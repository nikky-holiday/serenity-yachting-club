About the voyage

Sardinia and Corsica — from the water

The most beautiful shores of the two islands reveal themselves best from the sea. We start from Olbia and sail along the coasts, where the emerald water gives way to the pink beaches of the La Maddalena archipelago and the chalk cliffs of Corsica — far from the shore crowds.

  • The La Maddalena archipelago. National-park coves and pink sands you can only reach from the sea.
  • The Strait of Bonifacio. A crossing to wild Corsica and an approach to the chalk cliffs of the old fortress town.
  • Costa Smeralda. The famous emerald water and bays sung about since the 1960s.
  • Dinners in fishing ports. Fresh seafood, local wine and an aperitif on deck at sunset.
  • Wild anchorages. Spots with no hotels or crowds — just the sea, the rocks and your crew.
  • An intimate group of 6–12. Maximum privacy and a comfortable pace of travel.

Route

Day-by-day programme

Crew welcome, settling into cabins and getting to know the catamaran. An intro briefing and the first evening on board.

A crossing to the nature-reserve archipelago. Swimming in coves with incredibly clear water and the famous pink beaches.

A full day among the deserted islets of the national park: SUP, snorkelling and lunch at anchor in a turquoise lagoon.

A crossing through the Strait of Bonifacio. An approach to the striking chalk cliffs and a walk through the old town on the clifftop.

Granite islets of a nature reserve with crystal water. Swimming, grilled fish and the silence of the open sea.

A return to the Sardinian shore, a cosy marina, a walk and dinner in a fishing port.

Sardinia's most famous coast: emerald coves, a final swim and a farewell dinner on board.

A morning return to the marina, breakfast and disembarkation. Transfer to the airport by arrangement.
