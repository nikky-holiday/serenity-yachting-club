About the voyage

The Saronic Islands — quiet an hour from Athens

The Saronic Gulf is the ideal route for a first time under sail: short, calm crossings, a warm September sea and islands with character just an hour from the capital. You have time both to enjoy the sea and to feel real Greece without any rush.

  • Hydra. Stone mansions, galleries and complete quiet — motor vehicles are banned on the island.
  • Poros and Agistri. Swimming straight off the board in pine-fringed coves with turquoise water.
  • Aegina. Famous pistachios, the Temple of Aphaia and dinner on the fishing-port waterfront.
  • Easy crossings. Short, calm legs — comfortable even for those on a yacht for the first time.
  • A velvet September. Warm sea, soft sun and coves without crowds.
  • An intimate group of 6–12. You travel among your own, with plenty of personal space.

Route

Day-by-day programme

Crew welcome, settling into cabins and getting to know the catamaran. An intro briefing and the first evening on board.

The first crossing. A walk to the ancient Temple of Aphaia, the famous Aegina pistachios and dinner on the fishing-port waterfront.

Swimming in turquoise coves surrounded by pines. SUP, relaxing on deck and quiet far from the tourist routes.

A walk through the narrow lanes up to the clock tower, coffee overlooking the strait and an evening anchorage in a sheltered cove.

The most atmospheric island of the gulf: stone architecture, galleries, cosy cafés and complete quiet — no motor traffic at all.

A full day of sea: deserted bays, clear water, swimming straight off the stern and lunch at anchor.

An unhurried crossing towards Athens, a final swimming stop and a farewell dinner on board.

A morning return to Alimos marina, breakfast and disembarkation. Transfer to the airport by arrangement.
