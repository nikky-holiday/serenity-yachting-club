Club & community

More than a charter.
A community of those who value real freedom and a fine holiday at sea.

Serenity Yachting Club is a space for people who choose comfort, aesthetics and the thrill of real yachting. We don't offer cookie-cutter tours — we craft curated sea voyages from the scenic islands of Greece and Italy to the exotic Caribbean.

We take care of absolutely everything: from the careful selection of modern yachts and catamarans with trusted global charter companies to building the ideal route, choosing an experienced skipper and preparing every detail on board. Forming intimate crews, we make sure every voyage surrounds you with an atmosphere of comfort, safety and like-minded people you'll enjoy sharing the deck with.

All that's left is to step aboard and savour the sea.

How it works

Four steps to your
perfect yacht voyage

01

Consultation & idea

We get to know each other and discuss your wishes, comfort level and budget. You share your vision of the holiday — we pick the best sailing direction.

02

Choosing the yacht & route

We select a proven catamaran or sailing yacht, arrange an experienced skipper and fine-tune every day of the voyage — from quiet coves to seaside restaurants.

03

Format & company

Choose the format that suits you: book a cabin in a curated tour among like-minded people, or order a private charter entirely for your family or friends.

04

Turnkey holiday

We handle every detail: transfer, provisioning, insurance and logistics. All that's left is to step onto the deck and savour the sea.

The advantages of our service

Comfortable yachting,
where everything is taken care of

  • Ukrainian service & office. A transparent contract, 24/7 support and settlements in your native language — no hidden fees or complex terms.
  • A personal check of every yacht. We charter vessels and catamarans only from trusted global partners and only after a personal inspection. No "surprises" on board.
  • A holiday at your own pace. No storms or sporting exhaustion — only quiet coves, warm evenings on deck and full safety.
  • Intimate groups of 6–12. You travel among your own, without crowds or fuss, enjoying privacy.
  • Thoughtful concierge service. We arrange everything: from airport transfers and booking the best restaurants to private tastings.
